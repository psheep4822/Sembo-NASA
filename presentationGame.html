About the Game:

Welcome dear player!:

After decades of research, scientific breakthroughs, and technological development, humanity has finally reached a new stage of space exploration: the construction of a research base on the lunar surface. Designed to house scientific studies and evaluate the possibilities of future human occupation on the Moon, it represents a historic milestone in the history of space exploration. To kick off this mission, an international team of four astronauts—each a specialist in a different field—is sent to stay at the base for 10 days. Their responsibilities include conducting experiments, investigating lunar environmental conditions, monitoring the facility's systems, and ensuring they operate smoothly.

However, living and working in an environment so far from Earth will require many skills. You will be presented with various digitally illustrated scenarios, characters with distinct personalities, and missions that demand strategy, focus, and decision-making abilities. The entire situation will be controlled by you, playing as the Commander, who will carry out two mandatory missions daily over the 10-day period, along with a third mission randomly selected each day directly from the database.


Be careful with your decisions, players, as the lives of your crew members and the development of the lunar territory are in your hands!
