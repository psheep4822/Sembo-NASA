ABOUT THE GAME:

WELCOME, DEAR PLAYER!:

AFTER MANY YEARS OF SCIENCE AND NEW INVENTIONS, HUMANS HAVE REACHED AN EXCITING NEW STEP IN SPACE EXPLORATION: BUILDING A RESEARCH BASE ON THE MOON! THIS SPECIAL BASE WAS MADE FOR SCIENCE EXPERIMENTS AND TO SEE IF PEOPLE CAN LIVE ON THE MOON IN THE FUTURE. IT IS A HUGE MOMENT IN SPACE HISTORY. TO START THIS MISSION, AN INTERNATIONAL TEAM OF FOUR ASTRONAUTS—EACH AN EXPERT IN A DIFFERENT JOB—IS SENT TO STAY AT THE BASE FOR 10 DAYS. THEIR JOBS INCLUDE DOING EXPERIMENTS, STUDYING WHAT THE MOON IS LIKE, AND CHECKING THE BASE'S SYSTEMS TO MAKE SURE EVERYTHING WORKS SAFELY. HOWEVER, LIVING AND WORKING SO FAR AWAY FROM EARTH WILL TAKE A LOT OF GREAT SKILLS!

YOU WILL EXPLORE COOL DIGITAL WORLDS, MEET CHARACTERS WITH DIFFERENT PERSONALITIES, AND TAKE ON MISSIONS THAT NEED STRATEGY, FOCUS, AND SMART DECISIONS. YOU ARE IN CHARGE AS THE COMMANDER! EVERY DAY FOR 10 DAYS, YOU WILL COMPLETE TWO MAIN MISSIONS, PLUS A THIRD SURPRISE MISSION PICKED AT RANDOM FROM THE COMPUTER DATABASE.


BE CAREFUL WITH YOUR CHOICES, PLAYERS! THE SAFETY OF YOUR CREW AND THE FUTURE OF THE MOON BASE ARE IN YOUR HANDS!
