ABOUT THE GAME:

WELCOME, DEAR PLAYER!:

AFTER MANY YEARS OF STUDY, BIG DISCOVERIES, AND NEW TECHNOLOGY, PEOPLE HAVE REACHED A NEW STEP IN SPACE EXPLORATION: BUILDING A RESEARCH BASE ON THE MOON. THE BASE WILL HOST SCIENCE STUDIES AND HELP US LEARN IF PEOPLE CAN LIVE ON THE MOON IN THE FUTURE. IT IS A HISTORIC MOMENT FOR SPACE EXPLORATION. TO START THE MISSION, A TEAM OF FOUR ASTRONAUTS FROM DIFFERENT COUNTRIES IS SENT TO STAY AT THE BASE FOR 10 DAYS. EACH ASTRONAUT IS AN EXPERT IN A DIFFERENT FIELD. THEIR JOB IS TO DO EXPERIMENTS, STUDY THE MOON'S ENVIRONMENT, WATCH THE BASE'S SYSTEMS, AND MAKE SURE EVERYTHING KEEPS WORKING WELL.

BUT LIVING AND WORKING SO FAR FROM EARTH TAKES MANY SKILLS. YOU WILL SEE LOTS OF DIGITAL PICTURES OF PLACES, CHARACTERS WITH DIFFERENT PERSONALITIES, AND MISSIONS THAT NEED SMART PLANS, FOCUS, AND GOOD CHOICES. YOU WILL BE IN CHARGE OF EVERYTHING AS THE COMMANDER. EVERY DAY, YOU WILL DO TWO MUST-DO MISSIONS FOR 10 DAYS. YOU WILL ALSO GET A THIRD MISSION EACH DAY, PICKED AT RANDOM FROM THE MISSION LIST.


BE CAREFUL WITH YOUR CHOICES, PLAYERS! THE SAFETY OF YOUR CREW AND THE FUTURE OF THE MOON BASE ARE IN YOUR HANDS!
